Module 7 Setting up the survey

Learning objectives

How does QGIS work?

We learnt to collect data on QField but how do you create the survey in the first place?

1. Create a new folder qgis_training_example2 in the Documents section of your file explorer.

2. Create a subfolder named layers in the folder you created at the previous step.

3. Download the following files: Tree flags, Diameter values, POM values, Style, Fieldsheet.

4. Copy them in the layerssubfolder you created at step 2.

5. Open QGIS.

6. Click on the Projet menu and then Enregistrer sous….

7. Save your project under the name qgis_training_example2.qgz in the qgis_training_example2 folder created at step 1.

8. Click on the Projet menu and then Propriétés.

 

9. In the Général tab, verify that length and area units follow the metric system.

10. Click on the Couche menu, then Ajouter une couche and finally Ajouter une couche de texte délimité….

11. Click on the three points facing Nom de fichier. Another window pops-up, go to the layers subfolder you created at step 4 and select the first .csv file.

12. In Format de fichier, select the right separator. The table columns should be conserved, you can check in the section Echantillon de données. Verify that Détecter les types de champs is unticked. Verify that Pad de géométrie (juste la table) is ticked.

13. Click on Ajouter.

14. Repeat for all the .csv files in the layers subfolder.

15. Then go to the GeoPackage tab on the right-hand panel and click on Nouveau.

16. Select treesheet_training.gpkg from the layers subfolder in the pop-up window.

17. Click on the Connecter button, then on treesheet_training (highlighted in blue) and finally on Ajouter.

18. Click right on the treesheet_training layer and then click on Propriétés.

19. Click on Style in the left bottom corner of the pop-up window (it does not matter in which tab) and then click on Charger le style.

20. Click on the three dots symbol in the pop-up window.

21. Select training_style.qml and then click on Ouvrir.

22. You are now back to the previous pop-up window where you need to click on Charger le style.

23. You are back to the Propriétés window, click on Ok.

24. Your now have to format your QGIS project for export: click on Extensions, then QFieldSync, and finally Configurer le projet actuel.

25. Go to Export par câble and check that every layer is classified as Copy in the Action section.

26. Click on Extensions, then QFieldSync, and finally Paquet pour QField.

27. Verify that the QField package will be stored as closest as your computer root as possible, then click on Créer.

28. Connect the tablet to your laptop and allow sharing of the tablet data, then go to your QField package location.

29. Copy the QField package and paste it at the root of your tablet.

You can now proceed open your project on the tablet (see Using QField survey for a reminder on how to proceed) and collect data!
