How does QGIS work?

We learnt to collect data on QField but how do you create the survey in the first place?

1. Create a new folder qgis_training_example2 in the Documents section of your file explorer.

2. Create a subfolder named layers in the folder you created at the previous step.

3. Download the following files: Tree flags, Diameter values, POM values, Style, Fieldsheet.

4. Copy them in the layerssubfolder you created at step 2.

5. Open QGIS.

6. Click on the Projet menu and then Enregistrer sous….

7. Save your project under the name qgis_training_example2.qgz in the qgis_training_example2 folder created at step 1.

8. Click on the Projet menu and then Propriétés.

9. In the Général tab, verify that length and area units follow the metric system.

10. Click on the Couche menu, then Ajouter une couche and finally Ajouter une couche de texte délimité….

11. Click on the three points facing Nom de fichier. Another window pops-up, go to the layers subfolder you created at step 4 and select the first .csv file.

12. In Format de fichier, select the right separator. The table columns should be conserved, you can check in the section Echantillon de données. Verify that Détecter les types de champs is unticked. Verify that Pad de géométrie (juste la table) is ticked.

13. Click on Ajouter*.

14. Repeat for all the .csv files in the layers subfolder.
